Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions, Clear Answers

Everything you need to know about becoming a Referral Partner or partnering with MySilah as an institution.

MySilah is a UAE-based digital customer acquisition platform. We connect Referral Partners, individuals and professionals, with UAE banks seeking new customers. Partners introduce eligible customers to the platform; when their customer's application is approved by the bank, the partner receives a performance-based incentive payment. MySilah is not a bank, broker, or lender.
Any UAE resident above the age of 18 can register as a MySilah Referral Partner, including students, homemakers, working professionals, corporate employees, HR professionals and business owners. There are no educational prerequisites or professional certifications required to join.
Yes, joining MySilah is completely free. There is no registration fee, no deposit, and no monthly charge. You earn incentives only when your referrals are successfully approved by the bank or agency.
Payments are performance-based and are paid upon successful approval of a customer's application by the relevant partner institution, typically within 1–5 business days of the application being approved. Payment is transferred to your registered bank account. Full payment details are in your portal.
You can refer anyone in the UAE who may be eligible for a banking product, family members, friends, colleagues, neighbours, social contacts, business associates, or clients. The person you refer must meet the bank's and financial institution's own eligibility criteria (salary, residency, Emirates ID, etc.).
No incentive is payable on rejected applications, incentive payments are earned only on confirmed approvals. You can track the status of every referral in real-time from your dashboard. Our team can advise on improving lead quality for better approval rates.
No. Referral Partners on MySilah are independent third parties who introduce potential customers to products through the platform. MySilah Referral Partners are not bank employees, bank agents, or licensed financial advisors. You are not authorised to advise customers on financial products.
Customer data collected through the MySilah platform is handled in accordance with UAE data protection standards. Data is not shared with partner institutions without explicit customer consent. MySilah maintains a strict data governance framework, and all consent records are stored securely and are auditable.
MySilah provides partner institutions with a digital, scalable customer acquisition channel that delivers pre-screened, consent-verified leads. Institutions benefit from real-time tracking, comprehensive reporting, and a model structured with regulatory considerations at its core. There is no need for institutions to build or manage the referral infrastructure themselves.
MySilah operates as a customer acquisition and referral platform and is structured in accordance with applicable UAE commercial and regulatory frameworks. Our model requires all partners to adhere to a strict Code of Conduct. Institutional partners are encouraged to conduct their own compliance review of our platform documentation.
Yes. MySilah offers structured partnership models for SMEs, corporate entities, and strategic distribution partners. Business partnerships are available with enhanced reward tiers, dedicated account management, and API connectivity options. Contact our partnerships team for more information at admin@mysilah.ae.
Yes. MySilah is operated from Dubai, UAE and handles all data in compliance with UAE data protection regulations. Your personal information and that of your referrals is never shared with third parties beyond the bank processing the application. Full privacy policy is available on this site.
